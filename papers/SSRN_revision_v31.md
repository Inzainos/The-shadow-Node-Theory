## Title

Shadow Node Theory v2.5.2: Scale Invariance in the Node Satellization Algorithm
and a Universal Coupled Orbital Collapse Layer — Empirical Verification Across 721
Real Cases (Revision r31)

## Abstract

This paper presents Shadow Node Theory (SNT), a formal model of node satellization
across three scales of systemic resolution — Micro (Atomic Node), Meso
(intra-national Fungal Network), and Macro (superorganism collision) — in which the
satellization ratio follows a power law R(t)=a·t^b. The empirical base is a corpus
of 721 cases reconstructed from verifiable primary sources. Revision r31 follows an
internal integral audit that re-derived every figure: the arithmetic replicates
exactly, the inference layer does not, and several v30 claims are corrected or
withdrawn. Institutional friction is negatively associated with the exponent b in
every analysis variant (Spearman ρ=−0.68 per case, n=714), but the per-case
p-value is inflated by clustering and serial autocorrelation (domain-cluster
ρ=−0.56, p=0.25). The friction-free pole of the regime contrast consists of
COVID-19 spread data. The abrupt-vs-gradual trigger ratio (5.9×) is withdrawn: it
rests on 2 vs 2 cases. The HackerEarth churn model's ROC-AUC is corrected from
0.9994 (data leakage) to 0.715, and the Atomic Sovereignty Index "precision = 1.0"
is withdrawn as tautological. Domain B (country pairs, Maddison Project Database
2020) is reproduced byte for byte, but a discriminant test with a calibrated null
and Correlates of War bilateral trade does not support it as hub–node coupling,
and rebuilding it with trade-emergent hubs shows convergence toward the hub in two
of three countries. The paper keeps the Triple-Resolution Model, the N-body
analysis of Mexico (INEGI; b=−0.473, R²=0.838), and a Coupled Orbital Collapse
layer (ACO-A) with a falsifiable hazard h(τ)>0, a taxonomy of collapse modes and a
Principle of Least Friction, with real data in five domains. Falsifiability
criteria, a diagnostic protocol and a public replication package accompany the
model (Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.19446521).
